The office of the first fjord ferry

The official staff of the ferry office fluently filed the affidavits of the afflicted passengers, who had suffered a difficult crossing in the fifty knot wind. The chief officer offered coffee and waffles to the stiff and baffled travellers on the first floor.

Efficient staffing of the office keeps the traffic flowing, and the fleet of five ferries fulfils its schedule from fall to the first frost.

The historic stone church on the coast is a fact of the landscape, and the strict act of the old council still protects it.
