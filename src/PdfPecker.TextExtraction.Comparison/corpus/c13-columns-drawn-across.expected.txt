Harbour town plans a new tram line
The first trams will run in the spring of
2029, if the contractors keep to the
schedule that was agreed last month. The
route follows the old railway embankment for
most of its length, which keeps the cost of
buying land low and avoids the narrow
streets of the old town. Residents along the
embankment have asked for noise barriers and
for the trees that line the path to be kept.
The planning office says that most of the
trees can stay.
The national government pays forty percent
of the construction cost under its programme
for public transport in medium sized towns.
The rest comes from the municipality and the
regional health authority, which wants a
faster connection for patients and staff.
Ticket prices will match the bus fares, and
a single ticket will be valid on both for
ninety minutes.
